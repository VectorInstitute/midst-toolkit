## Categorical vocab threat-model assumption

- Prefitted `LabelEncoder` vocabs may include codes that do not appear in the
  training split alone (e.g. fixture pickle fit on train∪test, or a fuller
  mother vocab). That is **intentional**.
- Assumption: **all parties know the full set of possible category codes**
  (public schema / alphabet). Vocab support is not a membership secret.
- Membership boundary that *does* matter: supplied train/test **row** splits
  (`require_supplied_splits` / no silent resplit). Do not treat
  train-only-vs-full `classes_` cardinality as a membership bug.

## Prefitted encoder contract (Berka vs diabetes)

Same code path for both: `dict[column_name, LabelEncoder]` + `.transform` only (no refit).

| Fixture | Pickle | `transform` behavior | Why |
|---------|--------|----------------------|-----|
| Berka | `tests/.../tabdiff/trans_label_encoders.pkl` | **Identity** on coded ints (`1→1`) when `classes_` are contiguous `0..n-1` | Tiny slice already uses dense codes matching the pickle |
| Diabetes (test) | `tests/.../tabdiff_diabetes/diabetes_test.pkl` | **Densifies** sparse mother codes to `0..k-1` on many cols (e.g. weight `1,3,8,9` → `0,1,2,3`) | Fixture vocab is a sparse subset of the mother alphabet |
| Diabetes (mother / competition) | `/projects/aieng/midst_competition/data/diabetes/tabdiff/diabetes_label_encoders.pkl` | **Identity** on full encoded CSV | Full vocab; contiguous codes from the encode script |

**Do not swap pickles:** `diabetes_test.pkl` ≠ production/mother `diabetes_label_encoders.pkl` (vocab sizes differ; mother codes often absent from fixture `classes_`). Inverse sampling restores original (pre-densify) codes via `inverse_transform`.

Unit locks Berka identity (`test_prefitted_label_encoder_pickle_keeps_codes`) and diabetes densify + inverse (`test_diabetes_test_pickle_densifies_sparse_codes`). Do not “fix” densify to match Berka without updating fixtures/goldens.
